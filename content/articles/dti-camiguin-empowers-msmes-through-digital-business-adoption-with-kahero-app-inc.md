**The Department of Trade and Industry** – **Camiguin Provincial Office**, in partnership with **KaHero App, Inc**., successfully conducted the **MSME Business Adoption Workshop** on **November 12, 2026**, at the Training Room, Negosyo Center Mambajao, Poblacion, Mambajao, Camiguin.

The workshop was conducted in two batches, with MSME participants attending the morning and afternoon sessions facilitated by the KaHero App, Inc. team.

The activity aimed to introduce local entrepreneurs to digital solutions, particularly Point-of-Sale (POS) technology, that can help streamline business operations through efficient sales recording, inventory monitoring, transaction tracking, and sales reporting.

Through this initiative, DTI Camiguin continues to encourage MSMEs to embrace digital transformation, explore innovative business solutions, and adopt practical technologies that can enhance productivity, improve operational efficiency, and strengthen their competitiveness in an increasingly digital business environment.

Empowering MSMEs, one digital step at a time.
