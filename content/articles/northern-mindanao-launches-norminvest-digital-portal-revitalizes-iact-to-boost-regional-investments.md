We are improving the ease of doing business for investments in the region!

On August 11, 2026, we joined key government agencies, local government units, and partners as we formally signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) in [Limketkai Luxe Hotel](https://www.facebook.com/limketkailuxehotel?__cft__[0]=AZjNVCqgKfYh4u5STi62EVtxBiOCSJYZDAYptgicqc_3eUHfMzrOcTHZZcS6L0Qlhv1dJOq7u5oFhOxk9htwKJBlW2f7Kg0CagQEwdqVCXOHINayo4ah7FghwkhoT4DGZ0QCapLBU5g&__tn__=-]K-R), Cagayan de Oro City to launch the Northern Mindanao Investment Assistance Center (NORMINVEST) and re-establish the Investment Assistance Center (IACT) 10.

IACT 10 is the region's coordinating body that unites 35 partner institutions to streamline permits, handle aftercare services, and drive regional growth.

NORMINVEST is a new one-stop digital portal ([norminvest.dti.gov.ph](https://norminvest.dti.gov.ph/)) that centralizes investment information, incentives, registration requirements, and economic profiles for investors.

Explore the portal today at [norminvest.dti.gov.ph](https://norminvest.dti.gov.ph/)
