The Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) Bukidnon, through its RAPID Growth Project, in partnership with the DTI Bukidnon Negosyo Center, successfully conducted the two-day Youth Entrepreneurship Program (YEP): You Can Be Your Own Boss (BYOB) on November 11-12, 2026, in Malaybalay City, Bukidnon.

A total of 18 youth participants completed the two-day training, which covered essential topics on business development, entrepreneurship, business operations, and financial management.

Through interactive discussions and hands-on workshops, the participants gained practical knowledge and skills to develop their business ideas and take their first steps toward becoming future entrepreneurs.

The learning journey, however, does not end with the two-day training. As the third and final day of the program, the participants are set to present and pitch their respective business ideas on December 4, 2026, during the Madagaya Trade Fair in Valencia City, Bukidnon, providing them with an opportunity to showcase their concepts and apply what they have learned.

This initiative reflects DTI's continuing commitment to empowering the youth, nurturing entrepreneurial potential, and promoting entrepreneurship as a pathway to inclusive local economic growth.
