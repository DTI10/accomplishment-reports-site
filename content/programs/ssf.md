The Shared Service Facilities (SSF) Project of the Department of Trade and Industry gives micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) access to machinery, equipment, tools, systems and skills that they could not easily afford on their own. Enterprises use them under a shared arrangement, so that no single business carries the full cost.

By sharing these facilities, MSMEs improve the quality and consistency of their products, raise their productivity, lower their production costs and gain access to larger markets.
