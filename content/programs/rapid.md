The Rural Agro-enterprise Partnership for Inclusive Development and Growth (RAPID Growth) Project is a flagship initiative of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) funded by the International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD) that aims to strengthen agro-enterprise development in rural communities across the Philippines.

Through strategic investments, capacity building, and inclusive partnerships, the project supports sustainable enterprise growth and increased economic opportunities in rural communities.

## What the project does

- **RAPID Growth Network.** Links farmer organizations and agri-based MSMEs so they can grow as enterprises together.
- **Value Chain Development.** Builds up the commodities rural communities depend on, from planting to market. In Northern Mindanao these are coffee, cacao and coconut.
- **Rural Infrastructure.** Farm-to-market roads that connect producers to buyers.

Project-wide figures for all 7 regions are published on the [RAPID Growth dashboard](https://dtirapid.ph/webrep_v2/dashboard). To learn more about the project, visit [dtirapid.ph](https://dtirapid.ph/webrep_v2/whoweare).
